How to Play

  • You run forward automatically. Change lanes, jump and slide to dodge hazards.
  • Collect golden rings for points. Jump arcs of rings for bonus score.
  • Hold Accelerate to run faster. Hold Boost (uses energy) to rush through small robots.
  • Stomp robots by landing on them. The big Bulwark needs two stomps or a boost.
  • Grab the Velocity Core for a timed speed and jump boost. Green cells restore health.
  • Boss fight: dodge the warned attacks, wait until the chest core opens, then tap Boost to fire quill bolts into it.

How to play

Reach the finish gate of each level with as many rings and points as you can. Keep your five health cells — falling into pits and crashing into hazards costs one. Level 2 ends with the Iron Warden boss fight.

About the game

TURBO QUILL is an original 3D speed-platformer built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and three.js. You play Quill, a feather-crested courier racing through the Tropical Fantasy Island and the neon Speed Zone while the robotic Iron Warden tries to shut the tracks down. All characters, enemies, levels and music are original and generated in your browser.

Progress and settings are stored only on your device (LocalStorage). There are no accounts, leaderboards or online features.
